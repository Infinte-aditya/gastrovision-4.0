GastroVision is a free desktop research tool for gastrointestinal endoscopy videos. It reports which findings it sees consistently over time and highlights the image regions behind each decision. It runs fully offline on your own computer.

Research prototype – not a medical device. It has not been validated for clinical use and must never inform diagnosis or treatment. “No finding” does not mean a video is normal.

How it works

1Choose a video

Open an endoscopy clip (.mp4, .avi, .mov). Nothing is uploaded – analysis happens on your machine.

2Run the analysis

Frames are cleaned, classified by an EfficientNetV2 model and checked for findings that persist over several seconds.

3Review the evidence

See the verdict, a confidence figure, “possible” flags for weaker signals, and heatmaps of where the model looked.
