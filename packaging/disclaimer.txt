GASTROVISION - RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

GastroVision is experimental research software. It is NOT a medical device and has NOT been validated
for clinical use. Its output must never be used for diagnosis, treatment or any patient-care decision.
"No finding" does not mean a video is normal.

Everything runs on your own computer; videos are not uploaded anywhere. Do not send patient-identifying
information when you give feedback.

The model was trained on the public HyperKvasir endoscopy dataset (Borgli et al., 2020). Video decoding uses
FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), distributed under its own license.

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
